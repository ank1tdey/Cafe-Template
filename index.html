The menu

    Brewed small, served slowly

    Oak & Crema started with one secondhand espresso machine and a belief that a good cup should never be rushed. We roast in small batches, bake every morning before opening and know most of our regulars by their order.

    The room is built from reclaimed oak, linen and a lot of daylight. Bring a book, a laptop or a friend.

    Single-origin beans

    Roasted weekly from estates in Coorg and Chikmagalur.

    Croissants, sourdough and cakes, made fresh each day.
